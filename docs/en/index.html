Why Choose YouTube Dual Subtitles?

🧩 Smart Sentence Merging (Anti-Flicker)

YouTube auto-captions break every 2–3 words, causing standard translators to mistranslate and flicker. We purge [Music] noise, reconstruct complete sentences, and lock the previous sentence in Slot 1 while streaming Slot 2.

🔍 Word Lookup + 🎬 Authentic Audio Replay

Highlight any word or phrase on the subtitle to open a glassmorphic dictionary tooltip. Click "🎬 Play Snippet" to rewind and replay the speaker's exact original voice slice for that word!

⚡ R / A / D Shadowing Hotkeys

Built for active listening & speaking practice: Press R to replay the current sentence audio (auto-pauses at the end), A for previous sentence, D for next sentence, plus optional hover-to-pause.

🚀 45s Prefetching & 60fps Sync

Powered by a native main-world InnerTube channel and 45-second lookahead sliding window. Even at 2x–16x playback speed, bilingual subtitles appear with zero lag.

🎯 Respects Native [CC] Toggle

Never forces captions onto your casual or native-language videos! Dual subtitles only render when YouTube's built-in [CC] button is turned ON.

📱 Native YouTube Shorts Support

Automatically detects YouTube Shorts vertical layout and positions bilingual captions cleanly without blocking video titles or action buttons.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How do I activate dual subtitles after installing?

Open any YouTube video or Short with captions, and click the native [CC] button at the bottom-right of the YouTube player (or press C). Dual subtitles will appear immediately! Turn off the extension switch anytime to restore standard single-language YouTube captions.

Q2: Which languages are supported?

You can translate any YouTube caption track into 10 target languages: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Vietnamese, and Thai.

Q3: Is my browsing data private?

Yes. The extension is 100% open-source under the MIT License and strictly adheres to Chrome Manifest V3 security policies. We never collect, store, or transmit your browsing history, video IDs, cookies, or personal data.
